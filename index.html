THE STORY BEGINS

Welcome to the Shelby Empire

Britain, 1919. In the aftermath of the Great War, the streets of Birmingham are filled with poverty, political unrest, and criminal ambition.

At the centre of this turbulent world stands the Shelby family, led by the ambitious and calculating war veteran Thomas Shelby.

Their gang, known as the Peaky Blinders, begins as a powerful street organisation. But Thomas has ambitions far beyond Birmingham's streets.

As rival gangs, political forces, and determined authorities close in, the Shelby family must fight to protect everything they have built.
